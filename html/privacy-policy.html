Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2025

Effective date: November 16, 2025

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Music Files and Metadata

We store the music files and metadata you upload to the app locally on your device. This includes:

  • Song titles, artists, albums, genres
  • Audio file data
  • Playlists and user-created collections
  • Playback history and preferences

1.2 Usage Analytics (Optional)

With your consent, we may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app:

  • App usage patterns and feature usage
  • Error reports and crash data
  • Performance metrics

1.3 Device Information

We may collect technical information about your device:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Browser type and version
  • Screen resolution and capabilities
  • Supported Web APIs

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core Functionality

  • To provide music playback services
  • To store your music library and playlists
  • To remember your preferences and settings
  • To enable offline playback

2.2 App Improvement

  • To identify and fix bugs
  • To improve user experience
  • To develop new features
  • To optimize performance

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All your music files and personal data are stored locally on your device using:

  • IndexedDB for structured data
  • Local Storage for settings and preferences
  • Cache Storage for offline assets

3.2 Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures:

  • Encrypted storage for sensitive preferences
  • Secure Web APIs usage
  • Regular security updates
  • No data transmission without consent

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except as described below:

4.1 Service Providers

  • Lyrics services (with your explicit request)
  • Album art services (with your explicit request)
  • Error reporting services (anonymous, opt-in)

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Data Access

  • You can export all your data at any time
  • You can view stored data through browser developer tools
  • You have full control over your music library

5.2 Data Deletion

  • You can delete individual songs and playlists
  • You can clear all data through app settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data

5.3 Privacy Controls

  • Opt-in/out of analytics collection
  • Control notification permissions
  • Manage microphone and camera access
  • Control location sharing

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app's feedback system or settings.

9. Data Retention

Your data is retained locally on your device until you choose to delete it. We do not retain any data on our servers as all processing is done locally.

10. International Data Transfers

Since all data is stored locally on your device, there are no international data transfers. However, if you use third-party services (like lyrics APIs), their privacy policies apply.
